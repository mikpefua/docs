DRC Collection simplifies the process of receiving funds in the DRC, giving you reliable tools to collect money from customers across supported mobile money networks. Whether you're triggering a payment prompt or checking the outcome of a collection, DRC Collection keeps your inflow operations running smoothly.

If you want a hosted payment experience instead of a direct collection API, use [Checkout](/docs/products/accept-payment/checkout-overview). Our Checkout solution supports DRC payment methods including Mobile Money in both USD and CDF collections, as well as Apple Pay and International Card for USD collections.

## How it works

<Steps titleSize="p">
  <Step title="Initiate a mobile money inflow">
    Call the InitiateMobileMoneyInflow endpoint with the customer's phone number, the amount, and a callback URL. This sends a payment prompt to the customer on their mobile money network.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customer completes payment">
    The customer approves the payment on their end via their mobile money provider (e.g., M-Pesa, Airtel Money).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive webhook notification">
    Nomba sends a webhook event to your callback URL when the transaction status changes. Set up your webhook handler to process the result.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the transaction">
    Use the FetchMobileMoneyTransaction endpoint with the returned transaction reference to confirm the final status of the collection.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Reading the outcome

Every collection is initiated as `PENDING` and settles asynchronously, because the customer still has
to approve the charge on their phone. Two fields tell you where a collection stands.

<ResponseField name="status" type="string">
  The lifecycle state. `PENDING` and `APPROVED` are both still in flight; only `SUCCESS` and `FAILED`
  are final.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="gatewayCode" type="string">
  A stable, machine-readable outcome — `AWAITING_CUSTOMER_APPROVAL`, `CREDIT_IN_PROGRESS`,
  `PAYMENT_RECEIVED`, `WRONG_MOBILE_MONEY_NETWORK`, `TEMPORARILY_UNAVAILABLE`, `PAYMENT_DECLINED` or
  `STATUS_UNAVAILABLE`. Branch on this. The accompanying `gatewayMessage` is for humans and its
  wording may change, so do not parse it.
</ResponseField>

<Warning>
  **`APPROVED` does not mean you have been paid.** It means the customer approved the charge on their
  phone and the credit is still settling. Wait for `SUCCESS` before releasing goods or services.
</Warning>

A few outcomes are worth handling explicitly:

- **`WRONG_MOBILE_MONEY_NETWORK`** — the phone number is not registered on the `topupVendor` you
  chose. Only you can fix this: send the number on the network it actually belongs to. Call
  [Fetch Inflow Providers](/docs/products/global-collections/fetch-drc-inflow-providers) for the
  valid values.
- **`TEMPORARILY_UNAVAILABLE`** — the payment was never attempted, so nothing was charged and your
  customer saw no prompt. Retry later with a **new** `idempotencyKey`. This is the second most common
  failure outcome, so handle it as a retry rather than a lost sale.
- **`PAYMENT_DECLINED`** — the charge was not completed. The mobile money providers do not return a
  reason for most declines; in practice the usual cause is an unfunded customer wallet, and the
  customer is prompted for the amount **plus the provider's fee**, not the amount alone.

See [Fetch Transaction](/docs/products/global-collections/fetch-mobile-money-transaction) for the
full field reference.
